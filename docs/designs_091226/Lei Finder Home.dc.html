3bFor lei shops: the Oʻahu pilot
Get LocalHawaiʻi Lei Finder
For customers

Get customer requests for lei, free, during our Oʻahu pilot.

Get Local Hawaiʻi connects people who need a lei with local shops that can make it. Tell us what you offer, and we'll send you matching requests. You set your prices and take payment directly.

Apply to join the pilot No commission, no card required.

What Get Local Hawaiʻi is

A free Oʻahu directory and request service for local lei shops.
Not a marketplace, not a middleman.
Customers find you, you deal with them directly.

How customer requests work

1

A customer tells us the occasion, date, location, quantity, and preferences.

2

We show them participating shops whose listing matches.

3

They choose up to three shops and share their contact details.

4

You get the request by email or text and reply to the customer directly.

Nothing is ordered through us.

What the free pilot includes

✓
A shop profile page
✓
Matching requests
✓
A monthly summary of how many people viewed your profile and sent you requests
✓
A listing on the participating shops page

Free for the pilot period. No commission, no card required.

What we need from you

◦Shop name, address, and phone
◦Lei types you make
◦Occasions you serve
◦Pickup, delivery, shipping, and arrival greeting options
◦Areas you serve
◦Typical notice you need
◦A few photos
◦A contact for requests (email and/or mobile)

Prices and payment

You set your own prices. Customers pay you directly. We never handle money.

How we track results

We count profile views, requests sent, and requests you mark as won or not a fit, and share the summary with you monthly. We ask for a quick reply on each request so we can learn what kinds of requests are useful.

Questions from shops

Is it really free?

Yes, for the pilot period. No commission and no card required. If that ever changes we will tell you before it does.

What if I can't take a request?

Mark it as not a fit, or tell the customer directly. There is no penalty for passing.

Can I pause my listing?

Yes. Ask us to pause it for a busy season or a closure, and we will stop sending requests until you say otherwise.

How do customers contact me?

By phone or by the request we forward to your email or mobile. Your profile also shows your own call, website, and directions links.

Do you take a cut?

No. You set your prices, the customer pays you, and we never handle money.

How matches work

We match on what shops have listed: the lei types they make, the occasions they serve, their service area, and whether they offer pickup, delivery, or arrival greetings. We do not check availability or price. The shop confirms both with you directly.

A lei is given, not just bought

Lei carry meaning, and the right one depends on the occasion and the person. Maile and white flowers are traditional for particular moments. Lei are given with a greeting and treated with care. If you are not sure what is appropriate, say so in your request and the shop can advise.

Read the full lei guide

Common questions

How far ahead should I order?

It depends on the shop and the season. Give your date in the request and each shop will tell you whether they can take it. Graduation and May Day weeks fill up early.

What does a lei cost?

Each shop sets its own prices and will quote you directly. Your budget in the request helps them suggest the right option.

Do I pay Get Local?

No. You pay the shop directly, at their price.

Can a lei meet someone at the airport?

Some shops offer arrival greetings. Choose "Arrival lei greeting" and the shop will ask for flight details.

What if no shop can fill it?

We'll tell you on the results page and suggest what you can try next.
